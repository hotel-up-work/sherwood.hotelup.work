## About
Шервуд Stream is a hotel, restaurant and events complex located in Slobidka-Kulchiievetska (near Kamianets-Podilskyi, Ukraine), not in the city itself. It combines a restaurant serving Ukrainian and European cuisine, a banquet hall, live music, and on-site accommodation, positioned as a place where guests can celebrate, dine and stay overnight without traveling elsewhere. The page frames it as suited to weddings, birthdays, anniversaries, corporate events and family celebrations.
